Member welcome guide

Last updated 01/11/2026

The Rustnuts app is just for members: the ride calendar and RSVPs, club and ride chats, live ride on the day, your membership card and your fees. This page shows how to get it, how to sign in, and where things are.

Get the app

Open the link from your invite on your phone. The app is called Rustnuts SMC in the stores and shows as Rustnuts on your phone. It’s free, with no ads.

  • Android: tap the Google Play link in your invite. The page that opens says you’ve been invited to test the app: tap Become a tester, then its Google Play link, then Install. Use the Google account your phone’s Play Store is signed in with. If it says the app isn’t available, reply to the invite email with the Gmail address your Play Store uses and Jeff will add it the same day.
  • iPhone: tap the App Store link in your invite, then Get. The app is unlisted, so searching the App Store won’t find it; the link is the only way in.

Sign in

There are no passwords. You sign in once and stay signed in; only Sign out ends it.

  1. Open Rustnuts and tap Sign in.
  2. Type the email address the club has for you and tap Email me a code.
  3. Open the email from the club and type the 6-digit code into the app, then tap Sign in. The code lasts an hour. If it hasn’t arrived, check your junk folder or tap Send a new code, and use the newest one.
  4. When the app asks to send you notifications, tap Allow. That’s how you hear about chat messages and ride changes.

If you land on a screen called Ask to join, the app doesn’t know that email. Don’t tap Send my request: that starts a new application instead of opening your record. Tap Sign out at the bottom and sign in again with the address the club has for you. Still landing there? Reply to the invite email (or email apps@rustnutssmc.com.au) with the address you signed in with, and Jeff will fix it so you can sign in again.

Where things are

Five tabs run along the bottom of the screen. Board members get a sixth, Board.

TabWhat’s there
HomeYour welcome, today’s meeting or ride check-in, fees due, and shout-outs like birthdays
ChatClub, chapter and ride chats, with photos, videos and voice notes. A red number means unread messages
RidesThe ride calendar month by month. Tap a ride to RSVP and see the route, briefing and weather. Also the route library, the Rusty Bus and invites from other clubs
MembersThe club roster: road names, chapters, roles and bikes
MeYour membership card, emergency contacts, garage, fees, privacy and notification settings, Road Rules, and Sign out

First five minutes

  • Add an emergency contact: Me › Emergency contacts. You, your chapter board and the national office-bearers see it; on ride day the Road Captain uses it if something goes wrong.
  • Add your bike: Me › Garage › Add a bike.
  • Sign the Road Rules if the app asks you to.
  • RSVP to the next ride: Rides, tap the ride.
  • Check your fees: on Home or Me › Fees. Pay by PayID or bank transfer using the details and reference shown there, then tap I’ve paid this.

On ride day

Open the ride in Rides for the briefing, the route and the weather, and check in when you arrive.

Live ride lets the riders on the ride see each other, so the Road Captain and Tail Gunner can keep the group together.

  • Tap Open live ride, then Share my location. When your phone asks, choose Allow all the time, so it keeps working with the phone in your pocket and the screen off.
  • Only riders sharing on that ride can see you. It switches itself off when the ride ends, and only your latest position is kept, deleted when you stop. Tap Stop sharing, then Stop now, whenever you like, or Not now to skip it.
  • Once you’re in, the live ride screen also has Emergency (tap it, then Send emergency), I’ve dropped out and Call 000. Ride leaders also get Set regroup pin here.

Ride comms is hands-free voice with the other riders on the ride, and it works with the screen off. Tap Connect to join and Disconnect when you’re done.

Help and privacy

  • Stuck or wrong details? Ask your chapter secretary, or email apps@rustnutssmc.com.au.
  • Your data stays with the club. No ads and no tracking. Your chapter board can always see your mobile number; other members only see it if you turn on Me › Privacy › Share my mobile with members. The full privacy policy is also in the app.
  • Leaving? Me › Delete my account, or use the account deletion page.
