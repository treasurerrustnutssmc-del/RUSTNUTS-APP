Delete your app account

This page now lives at www.rustnutssmc.com.au/app/delete-account.html.

This is for the Rustnuts SMC members’ app, run by Rustnuts Social Motorcycle Club.

In the app

  1. Open the app and go to Me.
  2. Open Notifications and privacy and tap Delete my account (below Privacy).
  3. On the next screen, tap Delete my account again, then Yes, delete it for good.

Family members: it’s at the bottom of Me. Applicants: it’s at the bottom of the screen that says your request is waiting, or of the Ask to join form.

If you hold a board or national seat, step down first so the club can fill it.

Without the app

Email apps@rustnutssmc.com.au from the email address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your app account. We’ll do it within 30 days and reply when it’s done.

What’s deleted

  • Your sign-in (email address)
  • Contact details, licence and first aid dates
  • Emergency contacts, bikes and registrations
  • Chat messages and reactions
  • Ride sign-ups, crew spots, ratings and charity sign-ups
  • Swap and sell listings, build threads, bragging rights nominations, skills and birthday
  • Notification settings and phone registrations

Nobody can open the photos from what’s deleted, and we clear the files themselves.

What the club keeps

  • If the club loaded your place from its member roster: the roster keeps your name, email, membership level and dates, so your membership history stands and a deleted account can’t be used to take that place back without the board
  • Fees, payments and the club’s books
  • Copies of your club shop orders in the club’s order system (Make.com, Google Sheets, Docs and Drive, and Resend), as financial records
  • Meeting minutes, votes and action items
  • Attendance, status changes, notices and conduct records
  • Board seats held in the past
  • Rusty Bus bookings and logs, routes you uploaded and past merch orders
  • Anything someone has reported (a chat message, a post or your profile), as it was when reported, for the board to deal with
  • If you’re on the website photo crew: photos you put on the club website straight from your phone (take them down first if you want them gone)

The roster, the shop order copies and anything reported keep your name. Everything else here shows as “Former member” in the app; names typed into free text (for example minutes) aren’t changed. An incorporated association has to keep its financial records and minutes.

Delete some of your data and keep your account

  • Remove a message of yours in any chat you can still open: tap it (for a photo or video, tap beside the picture, not on it), then Remove my message, then Yes, remove it. That removes its text and reactions and hides any photo, video or voice note in it; email us below if you want those files deleted too
  • Take down a photo of yours that’s on the club website: on the Chat tab, tap the photos button (Photos and ride walls), then Website photos, then Take down, then Yes, take it down
  • Remove bikes and emergency contacts, and change or clear your profile details, on Me under My details
  • Stop sharing your birthday: on Me, open My details, then under Social tap Birthday shout-out, then Stop sharing it

For anything else, email apps@rustnutssmc.com.au from the email address you sign in with and say what you’d like deleted, for example a delivery address saved from a shop order. We’ll do it within 30 days and reply when it’s done.

What isn’t changed

Your club membership. Deleting the app account isn’t resigning from the club: talk to your chapter Secretary for that.
