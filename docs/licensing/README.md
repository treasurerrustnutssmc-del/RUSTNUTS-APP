# Software ownership and licensing

## Repository licence and signed-agreement record

The [root LICENSE](../../LICENSE) identifies Jeff Ostler's rights only in original
application material in which those rights subsist and are owned by him. It grants
Rustnuts Social Motorcycle Club Incorporated permanent, non-exclusive, worldwide,
irrevocable use for club purposes without a licence fee, including source copies,
builds, hosting, modification, distribution to users and replacement maintainers.
Jeff retains his software rights. The grant does not depend on executing another
agreement or completing a schedule.
Jeff controls the main application build and will maintain it for as long as he
is able. The Club may choose anyone to maintain its implementation at any time,
without Jeff's further approval or waiting until he is unable to maintain it.
Jeff may reuse the software rights he owns to build and license separate versions
for other clubs or customers. That does not reduce Rustnuts' permanent fee-free
rights, source access, choice of maintainer or Jeff's maintenance undertaking.
Club assets, member information and other separately owned materials are excluded
unless separately authorised. Reconcile this permission with the signed originals.

This revision replaces the root Expo MIT text with that scoped application licence
and preserves the former text byte-for-byte in [licenses/Expo-MIT.txt](../../licenses/Expo-MIT.txt).
It does not revoke or narrow valid earlier permissions, including any applicable
MIT grants over original code. The historical scope of the former root notice is
unresolved. The first revision containing the replacement marks the notice change;
it is not a retrospective restriction on earlier licensed copies.

Third-party materials, separately owned contributions, club branding/documents,
member content, personal information and infrastructure accounts remain separate.
Jeff confirmed on 8 November 2026 that the club chairman, sitting presidents and
treasurers signed the agreement and assignment. He described the arrangement as
Jeff retaining control of the app build, the Club having permanent fee-free use
and freedom to choose anyone to maintain it, and Jeff maintaining it while able.
This records his latest confirmation of signatures. The signed originals,
signatory identities, execution dates and precise assigned contributions were
not supplied for this review. Their terms and scope must be checked from the
originals; this record does not infer that every contributor's rights were assigned.
The repository agreement and assignment texts are **review drafts for reconciliation
with the signed originals**, not copies of executed instruments. They impose no
additional conditions on the root club grant or amendments to existing agreements.

## Outstanding evidence and agreement work

- Identify protected original contributions and their actual rights holders,
  considering employment/contractor arrangements, prior descriptions and AI-assisted
  provenance. Check the reported signed assignment's actual coverage and obtain
  any further assignment only where needed;
  a commit or pull request does not transfer a contributor's copyright.
- Obtain legal review of the historical MIT notice, distributed copies and GitHub
  contribution/platform terms. Ownership and permissive licensing can coexist.
  The root licence expressly preserves valid existing grants.
- Reconcile the signed originals with these review drafts, including the Club's
  registered identity, execution authority, signatories and handling of Jeff's
  personal interest. Identify any further agreement actually needed. Review
  consideration/deed form, enforceability and remedies
  without making the existing club grant dependent on those later steps.
- Complete the component/asset and actual release notice review, including native
  dependencies, fonts and icons. The JavaScript lockfile alone is insufficient.
- Verify source copies, build instructions, account control, costs and authorised
  access so the Club can operate independently. Keep credentials, member data and
  executed signature pages in authorised private storage.

## Maintaining this record

Keep the root licence, README, product purpose and collaborator brief consistent.
Record later verified assignments and covered contributions in the ownership
schedule; contributions validly assigned to Jeff for this application automatically
receive the root club licence. Do not mark an unresolved matter as legally settled.
Any later signed agreement must be accurately described and must not silently
withdraw the root grant or other valid existing rights.

Package licence metadata points to the root licence. Author credits remain credits,
not proof of ownership. Before a release, reconcile its actual components, preserve
required notices and satisfy applicable source/attribution obligations. This work
does not establish that any released app or website is fully compliant.
