## 1. Ownership retained

Jeff retains all intellectual property rights that he owns in the Original Software. “Original Software” means original application code and original technical documentation identified in the ownership schedule, in which relevant rights subsist and are owned by Jeff. No ownership in that material is transferred to the Club by this agreement, by access to the repository, by keeping a source copy or by operating or paying for infrastructure. This clause does not assign or extinguish rights belonging to another person.

Jeff retains control of the main application build and its development. This does not restrict the Club's rights to maintain or modify its own implementation or choose its maintainers.

## 2. Permanent fee-free grant

Jeff grants the Club a non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual and royalty-free licence to use, copy, build, host, run, maintain, repair and modify the Original Software for the Club’s activities, including membership, administration, rides, communications, merchandise and public club information. The Club may distribute executable app copies and updates to its users and through app stores, and deploy its website. There is no licence fee for these rights.

## 3. Source access and replacement maintainers

The Club may keep a complete copy of the covered source, build instructions and dependency records. It may give source access to officers, volunteers and contractors solely to develop, host or maintain the Club’s implementation, with appropriate confidentiality, security and use restrictions. Such access does not permit independent commercial reuse or sale of Jeff’s original software. App-store operators and hosting providers may receive the permissions reasonably necessary to deliver the Club’s implementation, subject to the relevant third-party terms.

The Club may choose any maintainer at any time without Jeff's further approval; it need not wait until Jeff is unable to maintain the application.

## 4. Continuity

The root grant is perpetual and irrevocable. Any additional bilateral terms must preserve it. It does not end merely because Jeff leaves the Club, changes office, stops maintaining the app, becomes unavailable or transfers his rights. Jeff must preserve this licence when transferring his rights. Any remedy for a breach must respect the agreed permanent-use arrangement; the parties must not assume an unrestricted unilateral termination power. A solicitor should settle enforceability and breach remedies consistently with the root grant; this draft creates no power to terminate that grant.

## 5. Future work and boundaries

Original updates owned by Jeff and supplied for this Club application are covered on the same terms. Contributions assigned to Jeff for incorporation into this application are also covered automatically, whoever incorporates them. Record both in the ownership schedule. Any separately agreed terms for other work must not withdraw the root licence for material it covers. Jeff need not create future features. The Club may not sell or supply Jeff’s Original Software as a product for another organisation without his written permission, except as independently permitted by law or a valid existing licence. Jeff may license his own software elsewhere without Club branding, confidential material or member information.



## 6. Third-party rights and earlier permissions

Jeff may use the Original Software he owns to develop, operate and license separate versions for other clubs or customers, including commercially, without the Club's further permission to reuse his own software rights. Doing so does not reduce the Club's permanent fee-free licence, source access, modification rights or choice of maintainer, and does not alter Jeff's maintenance undertaking. No licence fee becomes payable by the Club because another version exists. Club branding, documents, confidential material, member content and personal information require their own rights and permissions and are not included in this reuse permission. Check the signed originals for consistency with this wording.

Third-party code, fonts and other materials remain governed by their own licences. The parties must retain required notices and meet applicable distribution obligations. Nothing in this agreement restricts rights granted under those licences, valid earlier licences or applicable law. This agreement does not decide whether any earlier repository copy carried an MIT grant over original application code.

## 7. Club assets and information

The Club retains whatever rights it holds in its name, insignia and club documents; other creators retain their own rights. The Club authorises Jeff and authorised maintainers to use those assets only as needed to develop and support the Club’s implementation, to the extent the Club can grant that permission. This does not permit their use in another product, marketing campaign or sale. The OFL font remains separately licensed.

Software ownership gives Jeff no independent right to access, use, sell, disclose or retain member information. Access must be authorised for Club purposes and limited to what is needed. The parties will document access, security, backups, retention, incident response and handover arrangements. Member and other contributor rights in their content are not assigned by this agreement. Privacy obligations and individual rights apply independently of any description of information as belonging to the Club.

## 8. Costs, support and practical handover

Jeff will maintain the application for the Club for as long as he is able. If he becomes unable to do so, the Club may continue maintenance through anyone it chooses, with its source, modification and use rights intact. This does not promise particular new features, response times or uninterrupted availability. The fee-free licence does not require Jeff to pay hosting, app-store, email, API or other service charges. Any paid services require a separate agreement and do not alter the permanent fee-free software licence. The Club must have the account access, source copies, build instructions and authorised credentials needed to operate independently. Record actual account control and cost responsibility in an operational schedule; do not treat software ownership as ownership of those accounts.

## 9. Modifications and contributor rights

Modifying Jeff’s software does not transfer his rights in the original material. Copyright in new additions depends on authorship, applicable law and written agreements; it does not automatically vest in Jeff. Check whether the signed assignment already covers the relevant addition and actual rights holder. Where it does not, obtain any necessary further assignment or identify separately owned material and an adequate licence. The Club’s right to maintain its implementation remains subject to those third-party rights, not to an invented claim of Jeff’s ownership of every future addition.
