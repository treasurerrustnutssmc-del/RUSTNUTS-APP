Delete your app account

This is for the Rustnuts SMC members’ app, run by Rustnuts Social Motorcycle Club.

In the app

  1. Open the app and go to Me (applicants: the waiting screen).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Yes, delete it for good.

If you hold a board or national seat, step down first so the club can fill it.

Without the app

Email apps@rustnutssmc.com.au from the email address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your app account. We’ll do it within 30 days and reply when it’s done.

What’s deleted

  • Your sign-in (email address)
  • Contact details, licence and first aid dates
  • Emergency contacts, bikes and registrations
  • Chat messages, reactions and photos
  • Ride sign-ups, crew spots, ratings and charity sign-ups
  • Swap and sell listings, build threads, skills and birthday
  • Notification settings and phone registrations

What the club keeps, without your name

  • Fees, payments and the club’s books
  • Meeting minutes, votes and action items
  • Attendance, status changes, notices and conduct records
  • Board seats held in the past

These show as “Former member”. An incorporated association has to keep its financial records and minutes. Your name is removed from them in the app; names typed into free text (for example minutes) aren’t changed.

What isn’t changed

Your club membership. Deleting the app account isn’t resigning from the club: talk to your chapter Secretary for that.
