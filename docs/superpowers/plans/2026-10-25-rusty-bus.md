# Rusty Bus booking Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** members see when the club van is free, ask for it for whole days, and get a yes from the board. A recovery for today or tomorrow is booked on the spot.

**Architecture:**
- Pure rules live in `src/domain/bus.ts`.
- The database has one read-only table, with an exclusion constraint that makes a double booking impossible, plus three SECURITY DEFINER actions behind one advisory lock.
- The live app reads it with a plain select. There are demo and live actions, with parity cases.
- There are three new stack screens, plus a row on the Rides list and a chip on the ride screen.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Expo SDK 58, React Navigation, Supabase Postgres, node:test (`npm test`), `scripts/test-db.sh` (`npm run test:db`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-rusty-bus-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- **Refusal messages** are copied word for word from the spec, with the curly ’. They are identical in the app and the database, and each is exported once from `bus.ts`. The two shared sign-in messages come from `signedInProblem` in `routes.ts`, as in briefings and Road Rules.
- **Migration:** one new file, `supabase/migrations/20261025170000_rusty_bus.sql`.
  - It contains no `drop` and no `delete from`. A foreign key's `on delete cascade` or `on delete set null` is fine.
  - It touches no existing function.
- **Shared files:** add to them only, never rewrite. The tab bar is unchanged.
- **UI:**
  - no `Alert`
  - `ConfirmButton` for Cancel
  - Rustnuts Stencil for labels, names, buttons, numbers and dates
  - theme tokens only
  - 390×844
  - inline errors in a live region.
- **Dates** are stored as ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` and shown as DD/MM/YYYY. "Today" is the Melbourne date.
- **Limits** count code points. The app trims with JS `trim()` and the database with `private.trim_like_app`.

## Review Focus
1. **Clash edges.** Bookings 01–03 and 03–05 clash because they share a day. Bookings 01–03 and 04–05 don't. A single-day booking clashes with a range that covers it. Cancelled, Declined and Waiting bookings never block.
2. **Booked straight away.** A recovery today or tomorrow by an ordinary Full member is Booked at once. A recovery in 2 days waits. A clash on straight-away is refused, not left waiting.
3. **Races.** Two managers saying yes to overlapping requests at once: one wins and the other gets the clash message. The advisory lock and the exclusion constraint both hold.
4. **Past days.** A booking whose last day is before today can't be decided or cancelled, but it still shows in history. A booking that started yesterday and ends tomorrow can still be cancelled.
5. **Missing people or rides.** A driver or booker who has left (null) and a ride that was deleted (null) still render cleanly ("A former member", no ride line).
