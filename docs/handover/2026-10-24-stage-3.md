- **Photos:** a club photo feed (never private chats) and a ride wall per ride.
- **Database:** `20261101000100_chat.sql` (tables, RLS, actions, `chat-media` Storage bucket, Realtime on messages) and `20261101000200_link_previews.sql` (7-day cache, written only by the Edge Function).

- **Rules:** `src/domain/chat.ts`, mirrored by the SQL and checked by 49 shared cases in `supabase/tests/parity.json` (121 cases agree in total).
- **App:** Chat tab with an unread count, chat screen, new private chat (with search), reports screen, Photos, ride wall, full-screen viewer. Message and Block buttons on a member, Ride chat and Ride wall rows on a ride.
- **Demo:** made-up chats and four drawn photos in `assets/demo/`.

## Gotchas
- `npx expo install` needs `EXPO_OFFLINE=1` here; the proxy blocks the Expo API.
- Tests that use today's date must use `private.club_date()`; the Melbourne date runs ahead of UTC from 13:00 UTC.
- In parity SQL, look up chat ids before `set local role`, or RLS hides them.
- The headless browser runs in UTC; screenshots need `timezoneId: 'Australia/Melbourne'` or day labels look wrong.
- On the web, `require()` of a picture gives an object, not a number. `asImage()` in `src/ui/ChatBits.tsx` handles both.
