## What it is
- **Rusty Bus** (from the Rides list or a ride's chip):
  - today's state: "Free today" or "Out today"
  - a **Book the Rusty Bus** button
  - managers get "Waiting for a yes", with any clash in amber
  - **Coming up**, grouped by month
  - **Yours**: your own bookings that came off, or weren't answered, in the last 30 days
- **Booking:** the days, purpose, driver, ride, notes and who booked it.
  - Managers get **Say yes** and **Say no** (with an optional reason).
  - Whoever booked it, its driver and the board can **Cancel booking**.
- **Book the Rusty Bus:** the days, purpose, an optional ride in those days, the driver and notes.
  - A live clash line names the Booked booking it hits.
  - The button reads **Book it** when the booking goes straight in, and **Ask for it** when it waits.
- **Ride screen:**
  - **Chips:**
    - "Rusty Bus booked" (green) or "Rusty Bus asked for" (amber), with the dates when they aren't the ride's own day.
    - "Rusty Bus still booked" (amber) on a ride that's been called off.
    - "Rusty Bus booked those days" (steel) when another booking already has the bus.
  - Crew managers get a **Book the Rusty Bus** link, prefilled with the ride.
- **The database can't double-book.** Two Booked bookings can never share a day: an exclusion constraint guarantees it, and a lock serialises the actions.

## Rulings made without Jeff (he can veto any)
- **Bus managers** are the NEC plus anyone with an open board seat in any chapter. Ride Committee seats don't count. The club has one bus, so any board can say yes.
- **Who can book:** Full and Nomad members, plus managers.
- **Booked straight away:** a booking goes straight in when a manager makes it, or when it's a Recovery starting today or tomorrow. Everything else waits for a yes.
  - The booker is recorded as the one who decided.
  - A straight-away booking that clashes is refused. **Book it** is greyed out with the clash shown.
- **Saying yes:**
  - **Say yes** is greyed out while the booking clashes with a Booked one.
  - **Say no** takes two taps.
  - A booking already under way can still be decided. Only one whose last day has passed can't.
- **A moved ride:** the calendar sync treats a moved ride as cancelled and adds a new one, so its booking is left on a called-off ride.
  - The booking shows "The ride was called off or moved." with a hint to cancel or book again.
  - The new ride shows "Rusty Bus booked those days" instead of the Book link.
- **An ask nobody answered** shows as "Not answered" once its days pass. The stored status stays Waiting.
- **Coming up** puts a booking that's already under way under the current month.
- **On the form,** the last day follows the first until it's typed, so a one-day booking needs one date.
- **Demo data:**
  - the charity booking sits on Ride for Kids while that ride is at least two days away, and otherwise 30 days out with no ride
  - today's recovery is driven by Diesel
  - Spanner's Waiting ask clashes with the charity booking
  - Spanner's Declined ask is from last week
